# 🌸 Hirgal – From Home Kitchen to Successful Business

**Hirgal** is a digital platform designed to empower women running home-based food businesses by providing business mentorship, financial guidance, and practical learning resources.

Our mission is to help women transform their cooking skills into sustainable and successful businesses.

## 🎯 The Problem

Many women sell homemade food but lack business knowledge, financial guidance, and mentorship to grow their businesses.

## 💡 Our Solution

Hirgal provides three simple tools to help women start, manage, and grow their businesses.

### ✨ Core Features

**1. SheMentor – Business Mentorship**
- Access practical business advice.
- Learn how to manage a small business.
- Get guidance on marketing and customer growth.

**2. FoodBiz – Financial Calculator**
- Calculate food production costs.
- Set selling prices.
- Track sales and profits.

**3. BizPath – Business Growth Roadmap**
- Access beginner-friendly business lessons.
- Follow step-by-step growth guidance.
- Learn how to develop a sustainable business.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To empower women with the knowledge, tools, and guidance they need to become confident business owners.

## 🚀 Our Vision
